Privacy Policy — Album Tracker 26

Last updated: May 2026

Welcome to Album Tracker 26. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user information.

1. Information Collection

Album Tracker 26 does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

  • No user accounts
  • No analytics
  • No advertisements
  • No tracking technologies
  • No personal data collection

2. Local Storage

The application stores collection progress exclusively on the user's device using local storage. No data is transmitted to external servers.

3. Internet Connection

The app works offline. Internet access may only be used for downloading updates through Google Play.

4. Third-Party Services

Album Tracker 26 does not use third-party analytics, advertising, authentication, or cloud services.

5. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

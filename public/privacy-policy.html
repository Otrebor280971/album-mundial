Privacy Policy — Album Tracker 26

Last updated: May 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Album Tracker 26 ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles user information and data.

1. Information Collection

Album Tracker 26 is designed to operate without collecting personal information. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share user data.

The Application does not use:

  • User accounts
  • Advertisements
  • Analytics services
  • Tracking technologies
  • Behavioral profiling
  • Cloud-based storage

2. Local Data Storage

The Application stores album collection progress exclusively on the user's device using local device storage.

No information is transmitted to external servers or accessible by the developer.

3. Internet Usage

Album Tracker 26 can function completely offline.

Any internet connection used by the Application is limited to platform-related services such as downloading updates from the Google Play Store.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application does not integrate third-party services for:

  • Advertising
  • Analytics
  • Authentication
  • Cloud synchronization
  • User tracking

5. Data Security

Because all information remains locally stored on the user's device, users are responsible for maintaining device security, including backups and device protection measures.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

7. Data Deletion

Users may permanently delete all application data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the Application
  • Clearing the app storage through Android device settings

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future to reflect changes in application features, legal requirements, or platform policies.

If future versions introduce online functionality or external services, this policy will be updated accordingly.
